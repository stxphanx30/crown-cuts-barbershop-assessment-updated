Privacy Policy

How Crown Cuts collects, uses and protects personal information when you use our website and booking services.

Crown Cuts Barbershop respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit our website, make an appointment or contact us.

1. Information We Collect

When you make an appointment or contact Crown Cuts, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, selected service, preferred barber, appointment date and appointment time.

We aim to request only information that is reasonably necessary to manage appointments and communicate with customers.

2. How We Use Your Information

Personal information may be used to:

  • Process and manage appointment requests.
  • Send booking confirmations and appointment information.
  • Contact you regarding your appointment when necessary.
  • Respond to enquiries submitted through the website.
  • Provide customer support relating to our services.

3. Booking Information

Information submitted through the booking form is used to process and manage the requested appointment. Providing accurate contact information helps us communicate important information relating to your booking.

4. Promotional Communications

Crown Cuts may offer promotional discounts, such as a first-visit discount. If you voluntarily provide your email address to request a promotional offer, your email may be used to deliver the requested promotional communication.

Promotional communications are separate from appointment communications. Agreeing to the booking Terms & Conditions does not automatically mean that you have agreed to receive marketing communications.

5. Email and Third-Party Services

Crown Cuts may use third-party services to support website functionality, including email delivery and calendar functionality. Information submitted through these services may be processed by those providers according to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website. However, no method of transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Personal information should only be retained for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including managing appointments, responding to enquiries and meeting applicable legal or operational requirements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including requesting access to information held about you, requesting correction of inaccurate information and asking questions about how your information is processed.

For privacy-related requests, please contact Crown Cuts using the contact details provided below.

9. Cookies and Browser Storage

The website may use browser storage technologies to improve the website experience, remember certain preferences and manage promotional interactions.

These technologies do not create a customer account.

10. Children's Information

The website is not intended to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. Where a booking concerns a child, the booking should be made by or with the involvement of a parent or legal guardian where appropriate.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our website, services or information-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
